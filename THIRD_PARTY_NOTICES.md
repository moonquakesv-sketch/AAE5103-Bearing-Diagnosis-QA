# Third-party material

The project uses Python packages listed in `requirements.txt`; their licenses apply to those packages. Installing a dependency does not transfer ownership of it to the course project.

The selected vibration recordings come from the [Case Western Reserve University Bearing Data Center](https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter/welcome). Original files are acquired separately from their official URLs and checked against the recorded hashes. Dataset provenance and signal-selection limits are retained in `data/records.csv`.

ResNet-18 architecture and pretrained-weight support come from TorchVision. The exact IMAGENET1K_V1 weight is acquired from its official PyTorch URL, which is recorded in `external_assets.json`. The pretrained weight's terms and its training-data terms must be considered separately from this repository's authored code. See [TorchVision's pretrained-model notice](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models.html#general-information-on-pre-trained-weights).

Knowledge-source quotations and citations are listed in `knowledge/k01/sources.csv` and `knowledge/i01/v1/sources.csv`. NSK and Timken retain rights to their source material. The distribution retains short previously selected excerpts and authored paraphrases, not complete original webpages, PDF guides or photographs. The source excerpt index records historical snapshot hashes and locators; the complete snapshots are not distributed.

DeepSeek is the configured provider for optional manually initiated live requests. The archived token-counter registration refers to separately acquired third-party assets; the distribution does not bundle the original native libraries or tokenizer source packages. The portable demonstration does not initialize that historical counter branch.

No new repository-wide open-source license is assigned by this packaging step. Authored project material and third-party material retain their respective rights.

The collaborator module was supplied as a separate project contribution. Its complete provided data are preserved byte for byte in checked-in compressed volumes; the three script copies only adapt file paths. Its exact training environment and trained checkpoint were not supplied. Dataset naming and code identify CWRU-derived data; per-record conversion provenance was not independently reconstructed during packaging.
